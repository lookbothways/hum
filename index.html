Levels are relative, not calibrated decibels: the evidence here is the timing, and how far the tone rises above the background either side of it. Detailed readings are kept for the last twelve hours and everything older is condensed to one entry per minute, which is what the levels export contains. Anything the microphone missed is written into the log as a gap, so the record never invents coverage it didn't have.
