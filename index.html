Analysis runs on the audio thread, so it keeps measuring with the screen off and the phone in your pocket. What it cannot survive is you switching to another app or another tab — Android and iOS cut the microphone off the moment this page stops being the one in front. Lost time is written into the log as a gap, so the record never quietly invents coverage it didn't have. Levels are relative, not calibrated decibels: the evidence here is the timing, and how far the tone rises above the background either side of it.
