Levels are relative, not calibrated decibels — the useful evidence here is the timing pattern and how far the tone rises above the background around it. Readings are saved in this browser, so you can close and reopen the page without losing the night. Keep the phone plugged in, in the same spot, and don't switch away from this tab: Android and iOS stop the microphone when the page goes to the background, and any interruption is marked in the log as a gap.
